# Sieve app numbers re-derived: termination+speed CONFIRMED, discrimination HONEST-NEGATIVE on v1 core

Paper III sec11.6 sieve numbers (720x/93.86%/307-plateau/17x-discrim) were proven in anti-contaminate OLD repos (no-copy). Re-derived fresh on CURRENT core (harness tests/sieve_measure.c, receipt G-SIEVE-MEASURE-2026-07-01.log @15698b0): TERMINATION confirmed (Dickson wqo saturates) but plateau=2 not 307; SPEED confirmed+exceeded (16.77ns/pair, per-cand p99 0.063us, 50us gate clear ~800x); DISCRIMINATION = honest-negative on the in-tree v1 encoder (intra/inter ratio 1.0-1.3x vs paper 17x) — the fixed-13-node/6-order-stat/componentwise encoder is magnitude-dominated, over-collapses. Paper's rich discrimination belongs to the fuller 60-node encoder in the gated old repos; reproducing in-tree = real build task, NOT a copy. Do not claim 17x/307 for the in-tree core.
