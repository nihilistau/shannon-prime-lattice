# T4 Frobenius pi^k of the model WEIGHTS is REDUNDANT vs OK_Q4B as a compression lever. DO NOT RE-RUN as a compression build. G-T4-WEIGHTS HONEST-NEGATIVE (2026-07-01). Pre-registered kill-test on 3/3 real gemma-4-12B tensors (L0 q_proj, L0 down_proj, L23 o_proj): OK_Q4B (per-32-block int4+f16 = 4.5 eff bits/w) relL2 0.10-0.12; Frobenius 'free' per-tensor scale at 4 b/w relL2 ~0.40 (3.3-4x worse to save 0.5 bits); per-row ~0.21-0.28; matching OK_Q4B fidelity needs the scale back at per-32-block (where the codec is IDENTICAL to OK_Q4B). Rank-2 residual lowers error only by spending MORE bits. The free-scale property that holds on Ring-2 EPISODES (G-R2-FROB sub-ULP@24b) does NOT transfer to trained weight tensors (per-block outliers/dynamic range). Resolves roadmap-vs-CLAUDE.md tension in favour of 'convicted redundant'. NO OVERCLAIM: refutes T4-as-weight-COMPRESSION, NOT the T4 exact-CANCELLATION property (real, needs Q8~18GB, buys auditability not compression). Boundary thesis extended to weights (as T2/Mobius in G-T2-WEIGHTS).
